# Changelog

## v0.3.0 — Grenades, first person and a squad that rides with you

- **First-person mode** — press **P** (or Settings → View) to switch between third and first person. In first
  person you see your own gun and gloved hands; aiming brings the gun up to the centre, and scopes work as before.
- **Grenades**
  - Hold **G** to aim with a visible throw arc, release to throw, **Right mouse** to cancel.
  - The **mouse wheel** picks the type: frag, sticky, flash, smoke or incendiary.
  - New grenade models.
- **Building damage** — explosions dent and scorch walls and knock chunks off them.
- **Your squad rides along** — when you get into a vehicle, your teammates run over and jump in. One mans the
  vehicle's gun, and the passengers shoot at enemies from their seats while you drive (except inside armoured
  vehicles).
- **Crawling** — a proper elbows-and-knees army crawl when prone, with a sideways crawl when you strafe.
- **Jungle Hunt**
  - Brighter, more readable nights, a soft outline on soldiers in the dark, and lights along the roads and at the
    objective.
  - The parachute drop steers you away from the sea and the map edge, and you land facing inland.
  - Less HUD clutter: names over soldiers only up close, shorter radio messages, and the HUD is introduced bit by
    bit at the start.
- **Fixes**
  - Enemies no longer get stuck on scenery, which could stop a Survival wave from ending (Military Base especially).
  - Tower snipers now shoot back. If a wave's last few enemies are hiding, they're marked on your map.
  - Floating pickup and station labels no longer fill the screen up close or in a scope.
  - Debris from explosions no longer flies across the map.
  - Small HUD fixes (combo counter, long callsigns).

## v0.2.0 — Port Savanne, cover and flanking

- **New Jungle Hunt map: Port Savanne** — an occupied harbour city with enterable buildings and furnished rooms,
  rooftops, markets, a harbour and parked traffic.
- **Adaptive city lighting and atmosphere** — street lights, lit windows, neon signs and fires that react to time of
  day, rain and power blackouts.
- **Enemy line of sight on the radar** — guards you can see show their view cones on the minimap, cut short by walls.
  The minimap rotation and the enemy markers were fixed, and enemies onto you are shown at the screen edge.
- **Stealth** — crouch run (**Shift** while crouched), quieter creeping, and a crouched player right behind a guard
  isn't seen. Enemies can see you, and be shot, through windows.
- **Cover system**
  - Take cover against any solid wall, car or sandbag pile (**Left Ctrl**).
  - **Ctrl** with the crosshair on cover further away runs you to it, bent low.
  - Firing from cover pops you up over low cover or out of a corner. At high cover you shuffle to the nearest
    corner on the side you're looking, and **V** switches sides.
  - Shots go where the crosshair is, even when the gun barrel sits lower than the wall top. The crosshair greys
    out when only your own cover would be hit.
  - Your aim is steadier while braced on cover.
- **Enemy tactics** — soldiers break for real cover the moment they spot you and crouch behind it. When you dig in,
  one or two of them flank round your side while the rest keep you pinned, and you're warned which side
  they're coming from.
- **HUD** — panels fade out when nothing is happening and come back when they matter (Settings → HUD auto-hide;
  hold **Tab** to show everything).
- Fixes: random enemy pop-in near the player and the camera shoulder while leaning out of cover.

## v0.1.0 — first public release

- Windows x64 build (download from Releases).
- Modes: Survival, Boss Rush, Capture the Flag, and **Operation: Jungle Hunt** (1–6 player co-op PvE).
- Jungle Hunt: free drop from a transport helicopter, a finite 250-soldier enemy force, stealth (takedowns,
  bodies, noise, radios and alarms) or assault, alert levels from local contact to island lockdown, hunter squads,
  vehicles and boats, optional objectives, intel, forward operating bases, the enemy commander and helicopter
  extraction.
- Squad AI with roles (Rifleman, Medic, Heavy, Marksman, Grenadier, Anti Armour, Breacher, Squad Leader), downed and
  revive, soldier customisation and loadouts.
- Swimming (treading water and breaststroke).
